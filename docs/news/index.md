# Project News

Release notes and announcements for FactionsUUID. Pick a release below for its
full set of changes.

## Latest releases

* **[FactionsUUID 4.7.0](4.7.0.md)** — July 26, 2026
    * Recoloring default translations, plus dues for charging members daily fees, rent for taxing claimed land, home respawn invulnerability, etc.
* **[FactionsUUID 4.6.0](4.6.0.md)** — June 28, 2026
    * A big expansion of upgrades, in-game upgrade management, expiring invites, contiguous claim limits, and more.
* **[FactionsUUID 4.5.0](4.5.0.md)** — April 10, 2026
    * Reworked warp GUI with dialogs, `if` tags usable nearly everywhere, renamable commands, and more `translations.conf` migrations.
* **[FactionsUUID 4.4.0](4.4.0.md)** — December 4, 2025
    * Copper chest protection, an overclaim reward fix, and version-check future-proofing.
* **[FactionsUUID 4.3.0](4.3.0.md)** — November 25, 2025
    * Minecraft 1.21.10 support, the move to MiniMessage, and many new placeholders.
* **FactionsUUID 4.2.0** — September 10, 2025
    * Moved warps to be an upgrade, quality of life improvements, and continuing fixes from the big update.
* **FactionsUUID 4.1.0** — August 30, 2025
    * Minor update adding a few commands, and fixes since the big update. 
* **[FactionsUUID 4.0.0](../four.md)** — August 12, 2025
    * The big update, substantially modernizing the plugin.
